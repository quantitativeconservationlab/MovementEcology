\ulnone Banding and encounter data for kestrel individuals and corresponding environmental variables. Banding and encounter data acquired from Bird Banding Laboratory FTP site 11 May 2017.  \ul \
\
\ulnone Definitions of columns:\
ID - unique record identifier \
sex - individual sex\
E_COND - was the bird encountered alive (A) or dead (D)\
E_WHO - identity of the individual that encountered bird\
dist_km - geodesic distance between reported banding and encounter locations calculated by the authors\
bearing_degs - bearing in degrees between reported \cf2 \outl0\strokewidth0 banding and encounter locations calculated by the authors\
\cf2 \outl0\strokewidth0 \strokec3 avg_ExtAg - average percentage of agricultural land cover in the 4 km wide strip surrounding the path between banding and encounter location\
extB - percentage of agricultural land cover in the 4 sqkm area surrounding the banding lcoation \
\pard\pardeftab720\sl280\partightenfactor0
\cf2 \outl0\strokewidth0 extE - percentage of agricultural land cover in the 4 sqkm area surrounding the encounter location\
diffAg - difference between extE and extB\
\
B_TAVG - difference in average monthly temperature in the banding year from average monthly temperature for the same month during baseline period 1951-1980 (in degrees Celsius) at the banding location\
\
B_TMIN - \cf2 difference in minimum monthly temperature in the banding year from average minimum monthly temperature for the same month during baseline period 1951-1980 (in degrees Celsius) at the banding location\
\
B_TMAX - difference in maximum monthly temperature in the banding year from average maximum monthly temperature for the same month during baseline period 1951-1980 (in degrees Celsius) at the banding location\
\
\cf2 dispType30 - dispersal type using 30km break point: 1= long-distance, 0 = short-distance\
dispType25 - \cf2 dispersal type using 25km break point: 1= long-distance, 0 = short-distance\cf2 \
\
B_LAT_DD_10MINBLOCK, \cf2 B_LON_DD_10MINBLOCK, E_LAT_DD_10MINBLOCK, E_LON_DD_10MINBLOCK - latitude/longitude of the center of the 10-minute block of banding or encounter location, including for those locations reported with a higher precision\
\
bearing_10MINBLOCK - bearing in degrees between the centers of the 10 minute blocks of the banding and encounter locations\
\
dist_km_10minblock - distance in km between the centers of the 10 minute blocks of the banding and encounter locations\
\cf2 \
E_WHO_BIN - identity of who encountered bird, grouped into two categories - Public (includes E_WHO Public or Other) or Researcher (includes E_WHO Agency or Bander)\
